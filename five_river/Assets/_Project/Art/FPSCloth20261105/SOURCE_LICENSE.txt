FPS sleeve surface correction, 2026-11-05

Art direction follows the Dabudong night-combat demo and rookie soldier
Oh Seok-gu: preserve the existing subdued olive uniform color and matte
cotton weave; add restrained, unequal fabric creases. This material is an
art-direction approximation, not a claim of an exact historical textile.

Existing source retained: Poly Haven Stretch Poplin
https://polyhaven.com/a/stretch_poplin
https://polyhaven.com/license

Source files already present in Assets/_Project/Art/SurfaceDetail20261030/.

M1_FP_Sleeve_AlbedoTiled_20261105.png is a Blender emission bake of the existing
stretch_poplin_diff_1k.png at the existing 2x3 UV repeat. It preserves the cloth color.
M1_FP_SleeveFold_Normal_20261105.png is an original tangent-space normal bake
from a separate mathematical cloth relief swatch. No external reference image
is incorporated. The physical production arm/hand meshes, proportions, bones,
pose, skin weights, camera and gun transforms were not modified by this work.

Bake source and reproducible script are saved separately in evidence folder:
FPSSleeve_20261105/M1_FP_SleeveSurface_NormalBake_20261105.blend
FPSSleeve_20261105/bake_sleeve_surface.py

Revision 2, requested after the normal-only result remained too smooth:
M1_FP_Sleeve_ClothGrain_20261105.png retains that albedo and adds restrained
original procedural fibre-tone variation, without incorporating the reference photo.
The FPS-only runtime cloth surface now receives short asymmetric ridge/valley
pairs in the visible forearm region. Original arm centreline, nominal radius,
joint positions, wrist-rim fit and hand connection remain unchanged. No stored
character mesh asset, hand mesh, camera, weapon or other avatar is modified.
